Privacy & Safety

How we protect you on Bazaar

🔒 You stay anonymous on every listing. Your name is only shared with the other person once you both lock in a price.
Your privacy, in plain terms

This is a sample privacy statement for the Bazaar prototype. The points below describe how the app is designed to handle your information.

🎓 Verified students only

We verify a valid university email before you can buy or sell, so you're only ever dealing with people from your own campus community.

🕶️ Anonymous by default

Listings never show your name or photo. Identities are revealed to each other only after a price is agreed and locked — never before.

💳 Secure payments

Payments are processed in-app. We don't store your full card details, and your payment information is never shared with the other party.

🛡️ Blocking & reporting

You can block or report anyone from a chat at any time. Blocked users can't see your listings and you won't see theirs. Reports go to your campus moderators for review.

📂 Your data

We only collect what's needed to run the marketplace — your listings, chats, and ratings. We don't sell your personal data to third parties.

This is placeholder text for demonstration purposes and is not a legally binding privacy policy.

Terms of Service

The rules of the marketplace

📜 By using Bazaar, you agree to these terms. They keep the marketplace fair and safe for everyone on campus.
Agreement to terms

This is a sample terms-of-service statement for the Bazaar prototype. Creating an account or using the app means you accept the points below.

1. Eligibility

You must be a current student with a valid university email to buy or sell. One account per person — accounts are personal and may not be shared or transferred.

2. Listings & conduct

List only items you own and are legally allowed to sell. No illegal goods, weapons, or prohibited substances. Describe items honestly and treat other students with respect.

3. Transactions & fees

Payments run through the app. Bazaar charges a small commission on each completed sale, shown before you lock in a price. You're responsible for meeting up safely and completing the exchange.

4. Anonymity & identity

Listings are anonymous until both parties agree on a price. You agree not to attempt to identify or contact other users outside of the agreed exchange.

5. Moderation

We may remove listings, or suspend or ban accounts, that violate these terms or are reported by other students. Campus moderators review reports and act at their discretion.

6. Disclaimer

Bazaar is a platform connecting buyers and sellers; we don't own or inspect the items listed. Trades are made at your own risk. The service is provided "as is" without warranties.

This is placeholder text for demonstration purposes and is not a legally binding agreement.
