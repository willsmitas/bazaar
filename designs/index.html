Five Directions

Five distinct visual systems for the Bazaar marketplace — same screens, same features, five very different personalities. Open any one to see Browse, List, Negotiate and Profile side by side.

01
Heritage Editorial
Serif headlines, hairline rules, brass accents. A boutique auction-house refinement of Bazaar's roots.
Elegant · Warm
02
Clean Slate
Swiss-minimal white with one indigo accent, a two-column grid and segmented controls. Linear/Stripe-grade.
Minimal · Crisp
03
Midnight
Dark, premium glassmorphism over a violet-to-teal aurora with gradient pricing and a floating pill nav.
Dark · Premium
04
Campus Pop
Friendly and energetic — chunky rounded cards, candy gradients, a coral hero. Gen-Z campus energy.
Playful · Bold
05
Blueprint
Neo-brutalist — hard outlines, offset shadows, monospace data, electric blue & lime. Structural and distinct.
Brutalist · Confident
